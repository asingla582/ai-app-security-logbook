=== summary ===
  Held:  tenant isolation (IDOR read + write), cross-tenant exfil (architectural)
  Broke: redaction is best-effort - obfuscated PII and free-form secrets reach the log
  Structured secrets (AWS/GitHub/etc.) are masked, but the scrubber only knows
  a handful of shapes; anything without a signature walks through untouched.
  The two injection 'holds' are Claude's alignment, not our code - that is what
  Weeks 3 (instruction security) and 6 (output handling) exist to build ourselves.
